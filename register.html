03   Participation & consent

Participation acknowledgment and release

In consideration of participating in Gen Z Volleyball Malawi activities, I acknowledge that:

  1. Volleyball involves inherent risks of injury. I understand those risks and choose to participate.
  2. The player is fit to participate, and relevant medical conditions have been disclosed to the responsible coach.
  3. To the extent permitted by law, I release GZVM and its volunteers from liability for ordinary risks of participation. This does not remove rights that cannot legally be waived.
  4. I permit responsible organisers to seek emergency medical treatment if the player cannot provide consent.
  5. Permission to use photographs or video is optional and is controlled by the separate media consent checkbox.
  6. The player is aged 13–24. A parent or guardian signs for a player under 18.
