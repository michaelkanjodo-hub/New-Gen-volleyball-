Skill Breakdown

Float Serve Mastery

A no-spin serve that dances in the air. Strike the ball with a flat, firm palm right in the center. Keep your contact point clean and follow through low.

  • Aim for the back 1m of the court
  • Toss 1.5m in front, slightly to the right
  • Practice 25 serves per session
Watch: "Float Serve 101" in the Coach Locker Room

Top-Spin Jump Serve

Generate power from your legs, rotate your torso, and snap your wrist over the top of the ball. The key is a high, slightly forward toss.

  • Drive upward, not forward
  • Contact at full extension
  • Snap wrist to create spin

3-Step Approach

Right-left-right for right-handers. The last two steps plant and launch. Load your arms like a catapult.

  • Step 1: directional, smaller
  • Step 2: big, plant both feet
  • Step 3: explosive jump + swing

Arm Swing Mechanics

Bow-and-arrow loading. Lead with the elbow, then snap the wrist over the top. Contact the ball at the highest reachable point in front of you.

Forearm Pass (Bump)

Lock your arms straight, thumbs side-by-side, platform flat. Bend your knees, not your arms. Move your feet to the ball, not the other way around.

  • Platform = locked elbows
  • Angle platform to target
  • Absorb the ball, don't bounce it

Digging Hard-Driven Balls

Get low. Get your chest over your knees. Angle your platform to lift the ball, not absorb it. Aim for 2-3m off the net.

Setting Hand Position

Form a "volleyball shape" with your hands — thumbs pointing back, index fingers forming a triangle. Contact with finger pads only, never palms.

Footwork to the Ball

Always move feet first. Get your body behind the ball, balanced. The set should be smooth, like a gentle throw.

Block Footwork

Stay light on your toes. Take small, quick shuffle steps. Press over the net, hands angled downward to direct the ball back into the attacker's court.

Reading the Setter

Watch the setter's shoulders and hands. Their body will tell you where the ball is going before it leaves their fingers.

Plyometric Power

Box jumps, bounds, and depth jumps build explosive vertical leap. 3 sets of 8 reps, twice a week. Rest 60s between sets.

Court Agility

Hexagon drills, ladder drills, and 4-corner shuffles build the foot speed needed for defense. 15 minutes per session, 3x a week.

Core & Stability

Planks, Russian twists, and pallof presses. A strong core transfers power from legs to arms in your spike.

Match Endurance

Interval sprints: 30s sprint, 60s jog, repeat 8x. Mimics the stop-start rhythm of volleyball.
